Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

This website is a small static shop site hosted on GitHub Pages. When you use the checkout, Stripe handles the payment checkout and may collect information needed to complete the purchase, such as your email address and shipping address.

How information is used

Information you enter at checkout is used to process the purchase, provide customer support, and ship the order. Do not enter passwords, card numbers, Social Security numbers, or other highly sensitive information into the soap-details field.

Payment information

Card and other payment information is handled by Stripe's secure checkout. This website does not display or store customers' full card numbers.

Third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts this website, and Stripe provides the payment checkout. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.
